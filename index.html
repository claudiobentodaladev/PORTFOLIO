About Me

I am a Computer Science student and an independent Software Developer based in Luanda, Angola. My work spans multiple areas of software development, including web applications, desktop applications, command-line tools, and developer tooling.

I am the creator of ENIDE, a multilingual programming language and educational ecosystem that enables people to write code using their own languages. I independently developed its lexer, parser, and interpreter, alongside its documentation, learning exercises, and supporting tools, including ENIDE CODE.

Beyond ENIDE, I have published software packages on npm, maintain public repositories on GitHub, and hold certifications in JavaScript and MongoDB. I continuously expand my technical knowledge through independent study and practical software development.

I am currently seeking my first professional opportunity in software development, preferably on a part-time basis, where I can contribute to real-world projects and continue growing as a developer.
